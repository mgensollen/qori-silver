Sterling Silver Care Guide

Daily Wear

Sterling .95 silver is built to be worn, not stored away. Put your piece on after applying perfume, lotion, and hairspray — chemicals in these products accelerate tarnish. Remove jewelry before swimming, showering, or exercising to avoid exposure to chlorine, salt water, and sweat.

Cleaning

For light cleaning, wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth after each wear. For deeper cleaning, use a silver polishing cloth or a small amount of mild dish soap in warm water — rinse thoroughly and dry immediately with a soft cloth. Never use abrasive materials or ultrasonic cleaners.

Storage

Store each piece individually in an airtight bag or the original gift box to minimize exposure to air and humidity. Keep away from direct sunlight and heat. A small anti-tarnish strip inside the box will extend the time between polishes.

Tarnish & Polishing

Tarnish is a natural reaction of silver with air and moisture — it does not mean your piece is damaged. A silver polishing cloth will restore the original shine in seconds. For pieces with gemstones, polish only the silver portions and avoid contact with the stones.
